### UX Assessment & Strengths
1. **High-Contrast Dark Tactical Theme:**
   * Uses tailored dark slate backgrounds (`#0a0e14`, `#111722`) with neon accents (Cyan `#00d2ff`, Red `#ff1744`, Green `#00e676`) optimized for 24/7 control room shift workers, preventing eye fatigue.
2. **Immediate Situational Awareness (Top HUD):**
   * Top telemetry HUD immediately communicates camera scale (50 / 80,000), 26 connected departments, live IST clock, and active threat status at a single glance.
3. **Animated Trajectory Replay Ergonomics:**
   * The glowing polyline with numbered waypoint badges and chronological timestamps turns raw detection data into an intuitive, story-driven investigative timeline for police operators.
